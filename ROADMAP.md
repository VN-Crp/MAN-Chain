# MAN Chain Roadmap

This is the proposed project roadmap. Each milestone should be considered complete only when supported by public code, test results, or other verifiable evidence.

1. **Q4 2024 — Project kickoff:** establish the vision, direction, and initial foundation for MAN Chain.
2. **Q1 2027 — Whitepaper:** publish technical documentation, architecture, tokenomics assumptions, and development direction.
3. **Q2 2027 — Testnet launch:** open the testnet for public testing and feedback.
4. **Q3 2027 — Ecosystem development:** develop the wallet, developer tools, and integrations.
5. **Q4 2027 — Mainnet launch:** target launch only after security, operational, and legal requirements are met.
6. **Q1 2028 — Expansion+:** expand applications, partnerships, and network utility.

These are targets, not guarantees. Do not announce a launch as complete until the corresponding milestone is verified.
