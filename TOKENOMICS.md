## Team vesting proposal
Team allocation is 58,000,000 MAN. Seven proposed unlocks occur at cumulative months 6, 13, 21, 30, 40, 51 and 63. Proposed tranche amounts are 8,285,714 MAN for each of the first six tranches and 8,285,716 MAN for the final tranche, totaling exactly 58,000,000 MAN. No token or vesting contract has been deployed.

Presale terms, staking rewards, annual burns, gas economics and DAO controls require separate specification, testing, legal review and public disclosure before implementation.
